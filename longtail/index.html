How to play

Each round has 5 categories. Give one answer for each before the 30-second bar runs out.

Any correct answer scores. The less obvious your answer, the more it's worth:

  • Everyday 10 points
  • Sharp 30 points
  • Rare 60 points
  • Legendary 100 points

If an answer isn't on the list, you can try another while time remains. Categories don't repeat until you've played them all.
