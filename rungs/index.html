How to play

Climb from the blue word to the green word. Each step changes exactly one letter, and every step must be a real word.

Example: COLD → CORD → WORD → WARD → WARM.

Par is the fewest steps possible. Matching par is a perfect climb. A hint adds the next step on a shortest path from where you are.

Every ladder is a new random pair, and start words don't repeat until you've climbed from all of them.
