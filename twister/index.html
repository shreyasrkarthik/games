Twister

Tap letters (or type) to fill the word. Tap a filled box to remove a letter.

How to play

The five orange letters spell a common word. Put them in order.

Any real word that uses exactly those letters counts. A hint locks the next letter in place. Solving with fewer hints keeps your stats looking good.

Words don't repeat until you've seen all 1,800.

Your Twister stats

Bars show solves by hints used (1 = no hints, 2 = one hint, and so on).
