Lockpick

Long-press (or right-click) a digit to cross it out as a note.

How to play

A secret code is made of different digits (no digit repeats). Crack it in 10 tries.

  • a hit: right digit, right place.
  • a near: right digit, wrong place.

The pegs don't tell you which digits matched. That's the puzzle.
