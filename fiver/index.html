Fiver

How to play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word.

  • A is in the word, in that exact spot.
  • A is in the word, but in a different spot.
  • A is not in the word.

Play as many rounds as you like. A word won't come up again until you've been through the whole list of 1,800.
